How to ask

Request deletion by email

This works for every Fuzon app, even if you've already uninstalled it.

  1. Email {{ _mail }}

    If you can, send it from the email address linked to your account, so we can find it.

  2. Use the subject "Delete my account"

    The buttons below fill this in for you, with the app's name included.

  3. Tell us which app, and how to find your account

    Include the email address or username you signed up with. Please don't send your password.

  4. Confirm it's you

    To protect your account, we may reply to check the request came from you before we delete anything.

Before you ask

Things to do first

Deleting an account is meant to be final, so take a moment to check these.

Download anything you want to keep

If you've stored files or photos in one of our apps, save copies of the ones you want before you ask us to delete your account.

Cancel any subscription in Google Play

Subscriptions are billed through Google Play, and deleting an account doesn't cancel one. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions.

Uninstalling isn't the same as deleting

Removing an app from your phone doesn't delete your account. To have your account deleted, send us a request using the steps above.
