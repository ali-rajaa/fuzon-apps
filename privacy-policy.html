1. Introduction and Scope

{{ site.company.legal_name }} publishes the following mobile applications:

  • Cloud Storage – upload, store, sync, organize and share files and photos across devices.

  • Smart Switch – Data Transfer – copy contacts, messages, media, apps and settings from one phone to another.

  • GPS Voice Navigation – maps, live traffic and turn-by-turn voice-guided directions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use, share and protect it, and your choices, whichever of the Apps you use. Where practices differ between Apps, we say so. By installing or using any App you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Apps.

2.5 Information we do not collect or do

  • We do not read, scan for advertising, or sell the contents of your files (Cloud Storage). Automated systems may scan files only for malware and illegal content such as child sexual abuse material, as required by law.

  • Smart Switch does not upload your transferred content to our servers. Data moves directly between your two devices over a local wireless link or cable, and we keep no copy.

  • We do not record audio continuously. The microphone is used only when you activate voice search or commands (GPS Voice Navigation).

  • We do not sell your location history or precise location data, and we do not store full payment card numbers.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • People you choose (Cloud Storage): files or folders you share are accessible to recipients according to the permissions you set. Anyone with a public link can access that content.

  • Your paired device (Smart Switch): the data you select is sent to the device you pair with. Only pair with devices you own or control.

  • Map, routing and traffic providers (GPS Voice Navigation): limited location and route queries, without your name, may be sent to licensed providers to compute routes and live conditions. Voice snippets may be processed by a speech-recognition provider, which we ask not to retain them.

  • Service providers: hosting and data-center, analytics, crash reporting, payment processing and customer support providers that process data for us under confidentiality and data-protection obligations. Smart Switch providers receive anonymous technical data only, never your transferred content.

  • Aggregated or anonymous data: may be shared with partners for traffic analytics and map improvement; it cannot reasonably identify you.

  • Legal and safety: where required by law, court order or valid government request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of users or the public.

  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition or asset sale, subject to this Policy or notice to you.

  • With your consent or at your direction.

7. Data Retention

App Retention
Cloud Storage Account data and files are kept while your account is active. Deleted files stay in Trash for 30 days, then are permanently erased. After account deletion we delete content within 30 days and from backups within 90 days, except limited billing and legal records kept as required by law.
Smart Switch Transferred content is not stored by us. Temporary cache files on your devices are deleted when the transfer completes, is cancelled or the App closes. Anonymous diagnostic data is kept up to 24 months.
GPS Voice Navigation Real-time location is used for the active session and not kept unless you enable trip history. Trip history and saved places are kept until you delete them or your account. Traffic location traces are de-identified within 24 hours. Voice queries are deleted after processing unless you opt in to help improve recognition.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards. Cloud Storage data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), with optional two-factor authentication. Smart Switch uses an encrypted, authenticated peer-to-peer connection secured by a one-time pairing code or QR token. GPS Voice Navigation communications use TLS, and account identifiers are separated from traffic data to reduce re-identification risk. Access to production systems is restricted and logged. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your device and credentials secure.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, which may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a portable copy.

  • Correct inaccurate data or request deletion of your data.

  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including withdrawing consent at any time.

  • Opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (we do not sell it).

  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, email {{ site.company.support_email }} or use in-app settings. Cloud Storage: delete files or your account in Settings > Account. GPS Voice Navigation: view, delete or turn off trip history and saved places in Settings > Privacy. Smart Switch: because we hold no transferred content, deletion applies only to diagnostic identifiers; you can reset your advertising ID in device settings. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request and will respond within the period required by law (generally 30 days).

11. Children's Privacy

The Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

In the preceding 12 months we have not sold or shared personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Authorized agents may submit requests on your behalf with proof of authorization.
