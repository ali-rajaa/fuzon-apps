--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} One Fuzon Know-How guide (/guides/). A reading layout, unlike the homepage and the app pages: a left-aligned hero with the question as the H1, a contents rail beside the article on wide screens (folded on phones), an "In short" card, the sections, one "Do it with " panel, the questions people ask, "Keep reading" and the app's black band. A thin reading-progress line runs under the header. Front matter: Required: layout: guide, css: guides, title (the , ending " | Fuzon Apps"), headline (the H1: the question people search for), crumb (short name for the breadcrumb), description (150 to 160 characters, used for the meta description and the cards), permalink (/guides/<slug>), topic (a _data/topics.yml slug), tone (the topic's tone), date, order (position on the hub), keyword (the search phrase the page targets; not shown), sections (list of {id, title, kind, brief}: the H2s in order), faqs (list of {q, a}). Optional: updated, takeaways (list), lead, og_image, og_image_alt. While being written: draft: true, noindex: true and words (planned length). A draft draws each section as a placeholder (guide-placeholder.html: kind and brief) and leaves out the FAQ schema; once written, the page body is the article (each H2 with the section's id) and sections only feeds the contents list. SEO (searchfit on-page checklist, playbook 13.6): one target phrase per guide, in the title, H1, description, first paragraph and slug; H2s cover the sub-questions; FAQ questions are the ones people ask; 3 or more internal links (the app panel, the app page, Keep reading, the hub). BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList and (once answered) FAQPage schema. Copy rules (STATUS.md 2, playbook 14): general advice that's true on any Android phone, menu names may differ by phone maker; app claims only from apps.yml; "free to download"; no ratings, reviews,
