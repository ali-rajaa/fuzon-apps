Apps for the everyday jobs on your phone

Fuzon Apps builds apps for Android phones, and for iPhone where we can, for the jobs people do on their phones every day. Keeping photos and files safe somewhere other than the phone. Moving everything across when it's time for a new handset. Getting to an address you've never been to before.

Each of our apps sets out to do one of those jobs properly, rather than trying to do everything at once. That keeps them focused and easier to use, and it means every app has a page on this site that explains exactly what it does and how to get started.

All of our apps are free to download from Google Play, and Cloud Storage from the App Store too. Some features are available as optional in-app purchases, handled by the store you got the app from, so you can try an app before deciding whether you need more from it.
