What to put in your email

The more we know, the sooner we can help. A few details make a big difference.

  1. Which app you're using

    Tell us the app's name. If you use more than one of our apps, say which one the problem is in.

  2. Your phone

    The make and model of your phone, and its Android or iOS version if you know it. You'll usually find this in Settings, under About phone (Android) or General, then About (iPhone).

  3. What you were doing

    The steps that led to the problem, in order. For example: opened the app, tapped a button, then this happened.

  4. What happened, and what you expected

    Any message you saw, word for word if you can. A screenshot often explains it best.

Common requests

Manage a subscription

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by the store you got the app from. On Android, open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Delete your account

Want your account and its data removed? Our Delete your account page explains how to ask.

Get our apps
