{%- comment -%} Decorative glow behind a hero (playbook 9.2 and Appendix G6): three large soft radial gradients in the logo's colours, drifting slowly, plus a faint static dot grid. Replaces the grey cloud texture in the template. aria-hidden and pointer-events:none. Plain divs with radial gradients rather than blurred SVG: gradients are already soft, and transform-only animation stays on the GPU. Each blob takes 19s, 23s or 27s per cycle, so the pattern never visibly repeats, and all of it stops under prefers-reduced-motion. On an app page the tone class swaps the three colours for the app's. Usage: first child of a container with .hero-glow-host; the real content inside it carries .hero-glow-content. {%- endcomment -%}
