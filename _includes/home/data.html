How we handle your data

The short version. Our privacy policy has the detail for each app.

Read the privacy policy

  • You control the permissions

    Each app explains what it asks for and why. You can change or turn off any permission in your phone's settings at any time.

  • Delete your data

    Delete files or your Cloud Storage account in Settings. Deleted files stay in Trash for 30 days, then they're erased.

  • The microphone listens only when you ask

    GPS Voice Navigation uses the microphone only when you start a voice search or command. It never records all the time.

  • Your location isn't for sale

    We don't sell your location history or precise location. Live traffic uses anonymous, combined signals.
